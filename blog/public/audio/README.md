# Original console audio recordings

Original console sounds: Sony Computer Entertainment. These are third-party recordings of the original PS2 effects, not a Sony-issued download or a BIOS extraction.

- `startup.mp3`: [PS2 Startup Screen - Sound Effect (HD)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5SoqjQR6eU), Gaming Sound FX. Range 0.38–10.88 seconds, preserving the complete startup and reverb decay. Stereo, 44.1 kHz, 160 kbps MP3; only silence trimmed and a 300 ms fade at the very end. No pitch shift, synthesis, or repetition.
- `move.mp3`, `enter.mp3`, `back.mp3`: short excerpts from the user-supplied [PS2 Menu walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HUyRfqJew1A), chaoreturnsforgood76. Source ranges 13.30–13.72, 16.92–17.72, 72.25–72.90 seconds respectively. Short edge fades and a 90 Hz high-pass filter; mono, 44.1 kHz, 96 kbps MP3.

All clips play once per interaction. There is no ambience loop. Startup is decoded before the visual intro starts; its natural reverb tail can finish over the menu. Skip, mute, page hiding, and replay cancel previous playback. No BIOS files, video frames, logos, or spoken content are bundled.
